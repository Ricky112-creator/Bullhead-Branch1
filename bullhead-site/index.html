Emali

See the menu

It started as one butchery on this street.

Long-haul drivers heading for Mombasa learned the name first — a light still on at 2am, a plate of meat that made the last stretch of road worth it. Bullhead One is where it began, and the light has stayed on ever since.

Bullhead was never built for people who already knew Emali. It was built for the ones just passing through, who weren't planning to stop — until they did.

Four counters, one roof

Walk in for one thing, you'll end up circling all four.

Meat

Pick your cut at the butchery, watch it hit the grill next door. Boiled meat, fry, matumbo, maini, stew — whatever you point at is yours in minutes.

Fresh fish

Sit-down tables if you'd rather not eat standing at the counter. Same kitchen, same fire, just with a chair under you.
